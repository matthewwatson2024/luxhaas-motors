The Beginning

Born from
Obsession

LuxHaus was founded in 2018 by former military procurement officer Marcus Haus and automotive designer Elena Lux, who shared one conviction: the HMWWV — High Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled Vehicle — was the most capable, most iconic, and most under-appreciated vehicle ever built.

What began as a passion project in a Texas warehouse has grown into the world's most recognized name in luxury tactical vehicles. We don't just sell Humvees. We curate, restore, reimagine, and deliver rolling legends — each one a collaboration between our craftsmen and the client's singular vision.

Our Craft

Built with
Intention

Every vehicle that enters our facility undergoes a comprehensive 180-point inspection and restoration process that can take up to eight months. We source only the finest materials — hand-stitched leather from Florence, billet components machined in Dusseldorf, electronics systems developed with aerospace-grade precision.

When a LuxHaus vehicle leaves our workshop, it meets a standard that has no peer. It is not better than other Humvees. It is better than any vehicle, at any price, in any segment. This is our promise.

Our Journey

Six Years of
Excellence

2018

LuxHaus is Founded

Marcus Haus and Elena Lux establish LuxHaus Motors in San Antonio, TX. First vehicle delivered to a private collector in Dallas.

2019

The Obsidian Series Launches

Our signature all-black conversion program debuts to critical acclaim. Waitlist grows to 18 months within 90 days.

2020

International Expansion

First international deliveries to UAE, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. LuxHaus enters the global stage.

2021

New Workshop — 42,000 sq ft

Our purpose-built facility opens in Houston, TX. 40 master craftsmen. Full metal fabrication, paint, and upholstery in-house.

2022

500th Vehicle Delivered

A milestone celebrated with the one-of-one LuxHaus Platinum Edition, gifted to our first client.

2024

The Future

Exploring next-generation powertrain options, Level IV armor integration, and our first satellite showroom in Dubai.
