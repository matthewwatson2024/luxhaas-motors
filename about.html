The Beginning

Born from
Obsession

LuxHaus Motors was founded in May 2026 by Matthew and Michael in West Lake Hills, TX — united by one conviction: the M1165A1 HMMWV — High Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled Vehicle — was the most capable, most iconic, and most under-appreciated vehicle ever built.

What begins as a bold vision in the Texas Hill Country is just the opening chapter. We don't just sell Humvees. We curate, reimagine, and deliver rolling legends — each one a collaboration between our craftsmen and the client's singular vision.

Our Craft

Built with
Intention

Every vehicle that enters our facility undergoes a comprehensive 180-point inspection and restoration process that can take up to eight months. We source only the finest materials — hand-stitched leather from Florence, billet components machined in Dusseldorf, electronics systems developed with aerospace-grade precision.

When a LuxHaus vehicle leaves our workshop, it meets a standard that has no peer. It is not better than other Humvees. It is better than any vehicle, at any price, in any segment. This is our promise.
